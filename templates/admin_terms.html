Protect confidential access

Keep administrator passwords, login sessions, recovery details, and access tools private. Do not share them or let another person use your administrator account.

Respect user privacy

Use personal information, certificates, account data, and reports only when needed for legitimate platform administration. Never copy, disclose, sell, or misuse user information.

Handle chats responsibly

Do not access, reveal, alter, or delete private conversations unless a valid safety, moderation, or support duty requires it. Keep all user conversations confidential.

Use moderation tools fairly

Review certificates, reports, and accounts honestly and consistently. Do not approve, reject, suspend, or change records for personal reasons.

Maintain a safe platform

Report security concerns promptly and use administrator access only to support a safe, respectful SkillSwap community. Misuse may result in removal of administrator access.
